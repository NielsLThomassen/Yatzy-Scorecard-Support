About the app

Yatzy Scorecard is a digital scorecard for 5-dice and 6-dice Yatzy. It includes automatic score calculations, multiple players, game history, virtual dice, and optional synchronization across your Apple devices using iCloud.

iCloud sync

If iCloud sync is not working, make sure you are signed in to iCloud on your device and that iCloud is enabled. Synchronization may take a short time after changes are made on another device.
