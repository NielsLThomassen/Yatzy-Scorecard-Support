Privacy Policy

Yatzy Scorecard · Last updated October 4, 2026

Yatzy Scorecard is designed to work without advertising, analytics, tracking, or an account operated by the developer.

Data used by the app

The app stores information needed to provide its scorekeeping features, such as player names, game scores, game history, and app preferences.

iCloud and CloudKit

If iCloud synchronization is available, Yatzy Scorecard uses Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services to synchronize game data between your Apple devices. This data is associated with your iCloud account and is handled by Apple as part of its iCloud services.

Yatzy Scorecard does not operate its own server for storing your game data.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Yatzy Scorecard does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. The app does not use your activity to track you across apps or websites.

Sharing of data

The developer does not sell your personal data and does not share your Yatzy Scorecard data with advertisers or data brokers.

Data deletion

You can remove game data from within the app where deletion controls are provided. Data synchronized through iCloud is managed through Apple's iCloud infrastructure and your Apple account.

Children's privacy

Yatzy Scorecard does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated service.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
